The Swiss Market

CHF 7 trillion.
Three structural barriers.

Switzerland is not a single market. A generic European strategy is not just inefficient — it is an expensive miscalculation.

8 Distinct Profiles · 8 Regulatory Realities
Segment Fragmentation
From CHF 2.7T Private Banking to heavily regulated Pension Funds and nimble Family Offices — each operates under entirely different frameworks and decision timelines.
The consequenceA one-size pitch reaches none of them.
4 Financial Hubs · 3 Languages
Cultural & Linguistic Grid
Zurich, Geneva, Basel and Lugano are distinct investment cultures. Treating Switzerland as an extension of the German or French market fails immediately.
The consequenceLocal language and localised networks are strict prerequisites.
>50% of Pension Mandates Intermediated
The Consultant Gatekeepers
Independent investment consultants control the flow of the majority of pension fund assets. You cannot reach the allocator without passing through them first.
The consequenceA dual-track sales cycle is mandatory — consultant first, then allocator.
